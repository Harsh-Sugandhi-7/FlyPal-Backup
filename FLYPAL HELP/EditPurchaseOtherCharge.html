Edit Other Charge

You can edit Other Charge information on this screen. For editing the Other Charge information, go through following steps :-

How to Edit The Other Charge ?

  1. Search a particular record & click Find Now button. You can Search the Other Charge from following way :
    • All : From this you search all the records of Other Charge.
    • Date : From this you can search the Other Charge by Date. You can search the Other Charge Date by All, Last 1 week, Last 1 Month, Last 1 Quarter, Last 1 year, Current Financial Year & Between Dates. For Between Dates criteria you have to enter the starting & ending dates of Other Charge.
    • Other Charge : From this you search the Other Charge by Other Charge Number. For searching this you have to select the Other Charge Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search. 
  2. Click Edit link which you want to edit the record, You get Other Charge Details screen.
  3. After editing click Save button, The updated record is added in the Other Charge List.

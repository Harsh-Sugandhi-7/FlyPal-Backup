Add/Edit/Delete Term

To reach the Term screen click Entries link, Select Goods Issue link, You get List of Issues screen. Click Add New button on List of Issues screen, You get Issue Details screen. Click Add button on Issue Details screen, You get List of Issue Term screen. Again click on the Icon of Term field, You get Term screen.

How to Add New Term ?

  1. Enter the name of Term & click Add/Edit button, The record is added in the list.

How to Edit The Term ?

  1. Click Edit link which you want to edit the record.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the Term List.

How to Delete The Term ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected Term, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

Terms screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add/Edit Add/Edit button to add/edit the term.
Back Back button to return back to the List of Order Term screen.

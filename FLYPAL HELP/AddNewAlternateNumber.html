Add New Opening Balance

You can add new Opening Balance information on this screen. For adding new Opening Balance, go through following steps :-

How To Add New Opening Balance ?

  1. Click Edit link on the Part List screen, You get Part Information screen.
  2. Click Next button on Part Information screen, You get Applicable Model List For Part No. screen.
  3. Click Next button on Applicable Model List For Part No. screen, You get Opening Balance Information screen.
  4. Again click Add New button on Opening Balance Information screen, You get Opening Stock Details screen.
  5. Enter all the information & click OK button, The record is added successfully in the Opening Balance List.
  6. After adding click Close button & then click Save button to save the record.

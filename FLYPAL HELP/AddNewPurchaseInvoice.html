Add New Purchase Invoice

You can add new Purchase Invoice information on this screen. For adding new Purchase Invoice information, go through following steps :-

How to Add New Purchase Invoice ?

  1. Click Add New button on List of Purchase Invoice screen, You get Invoice Details screen.
  2. Enter all the information in Invoice Details screen & click Save button, The record is added successfully in the Invoice List.

Note:
The Purchase Invoice can not be save without Invoice Items.

Add/Delete Aircraft

To reach the List of Aircrafts screen click Add button on Requisition Item screen, You get List of Aircrafts screen. 

How to Add New Aircraft ?

  1. For selecting the Aircraft click the Checkbox on List of Aircrafts screen & click Ok button, The record is added successfully in the Aircraft List.
  2. After adding click Ok button on Requisition Item screen to save the record.

How to Delete The Aircraft ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected Aircraft, The selected Aircraft is getting deleted from the Aircraft List.
  2. After deleting click Ok button on Requisition Item screen to save the record.

Note:
You can also delete the Aircrafts from List of Aircrafts screen. For that Uncheck the selected checkbox.

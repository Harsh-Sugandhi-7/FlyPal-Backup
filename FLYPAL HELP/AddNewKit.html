Add New Inspection Kit

You can add new Kit information on this screen. For adding new Kit information, go through following steps :-

How to Add New Inspection Kit ?

  1. Click Add New button on Inspection Kit List screen, You get Inspection Kit screen.
  2. Enter the name of Inspection Kit & it's Inspection Items & click Save button, The record is added successfully in the Inspection Kit List.

Note:
Kit can not be save without Kit Items.

Add/Edit/Delete Category

To reach the Category screen click Master link, Select Part link, You get Part List screen. Click Add New button on Part List screen, You get Part Information screen. Click on the Icon of Category field, You get Category screen. 

How to Add New Category ?

  1. Enter the name of Category & click Add/Edit button, The record is added in the list.

How To Edit The Category ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record to edit the information.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the list.

How To Delete The Category ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

Category screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add/Edit Add/Edit button to add/edit the Category information.
Back Back button to return back to the Part Information screen.

Add/Edit/Delete Unit

To reach the Unit screen click Master link, Select Part link, You get Part List screen. Click Add New button on Part List screen, You get Part Information screen. Click on the Icon of Unit field, You get Unit screen. 

How to Add New Unit ?

  1. Enter the name of Unit & click Add/Edit button, The record is added in the list.

How To Edit The Unit ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record to edit the information.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the list.

How To Delete The Unit ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

Unit screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add/Edit Add/Edit button to add/edit the Unit information.
Back Back button to return back to the Part Information screen.

Add/Edit/Delete Country

To reach the Country screen click Master link, Select Vendor link, You get Vendor List screen. Click Add New button on Vendor List screen, You get Vendor Details screen. Click on the Icon of City field, You get City Information screen. Click on the Icon of State Name field, You get State Information screen. Again click on the Icon of Country Name field, You get Country screen.   

How To Add New Country ?

  1. Enter the Country name & click Add/Edit button, The record is added in the list.

How To Edit The Country ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record to edit the information.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the list.

How To Delete The Country ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

Country screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add/Edit Add/Edit button to add/edit the Country.
Back Back button to return back to the State Information screen.

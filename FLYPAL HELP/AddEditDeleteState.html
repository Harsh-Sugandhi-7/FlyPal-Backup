Add/Edit/Delete State

To reach the State Information screen click Master link, Select Vendor link, You get Vendor List screen. Click Add New button on Vendor List screen, You get Vendor Details screen. Click on the Icon of City field, You get City Information screen. Again click on the Icon of State Name field, You get State Information screen. 

How to Add New State ?

  1. Enter the State & it's Country name & click Add/Edit button, The record is added in the list.

How to Edit The State ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record to edit the information.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the list.

How to Delete The State ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

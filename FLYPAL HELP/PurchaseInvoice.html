Purchase Invoice

To reach the List of Purchase Invoice screen click Entries link, Select Purchase Invoice link, You get List of Purchase Invoice screen. You can Add/Edit/Delete/Search Invoice, Invoice Item & Invoice Charge information on this screen.

What do you want to do?
  • Add New Purchase Invoice
    Click on this link to see the information about how to Add New Purchase Invoice.
  • Edit Purchase Invoice
    Click on this link to see the information about how to Edit the Purchase Invoice.
  • Delete Purchase Invoice
    Click on this link to see the information about how to Delete the Purchase Invoice.
  • Search Purchase Invoice
    Click on this link to see the information about how to Search Purchase Invoice.

You can Add/Edit/Delete Invoice Item | Add/Edit/Delete Invoice Charge | Add/Edit/Delete Charge on this screen.

Invoice Details screen contains following fields :

Field Name Field Description
No. Invoice Number is auto generated (e.g. INV-1999-2000-6).
Date The date on which the Invoice is being made (e.g. 04-May-2006).
Status The status may be Opened, Authorized or Cancelled. You can select the status from drop down list.
Name Vendor means to whom the invoice is made. You can select the vendor name from drop down list (e.g. Indian Oil corporation Ltd).
Invoice No. It is the suppliers invoice number (e.g. ENP/DA/01).
Date Invoice date is the suppliers invoice date (e.g. 11-Aug-2004).
Currency It is the name of currency (e.g. Rupees).
Factor It is the factor of that selected currency (e.g. 1.00).
Total Total=Quantity*Rate + Other Charges (e.g. 10.00*10.00+100.00=200.00).
Total Other Charges Total other charges depends on invoice charge. Total Other Charges=Total*Percentage/100 (e.g. 150.00*2.00/100.00=3.00).
Grand Total Grand Total=Total + Total Other Charges (e.g. 6131.00+1930.00=8061.00).
Remark You can enter any remark for this entry in the invoice.

Invoice Details screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add Add button for adding new Invoice Items.
Add Add button for adding the Charge in the Invoice Charge List.
Save Save button to save the Purchase Invoice.
Print Print button to print the given Invoice. We can print the Invoice only when it is Authorized.
Back Back button to return back to the List of Purchase Invoice screen.

Invoice Status :-

  1. Opened :
    The invoice is saved by default in it's open state. Until it is authorized it can not be printed. We can edit the invoice in open state only.
  2. Authorized :
    When the invoice is authorized then only we can print it. Once it is authorize it can not be edit.
  3. Cancelled :
    User can cancel the invoice but it will be saved with cancelled status. Once it is cancelled it can not be authorized or edit.

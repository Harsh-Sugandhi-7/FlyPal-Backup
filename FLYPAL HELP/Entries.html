Entries

Entries are the pre-requisites of the application. You can perform Add/Edit/Delete/Search operation in the selected Entries.
Entries are of following types :-

  • Enquiry
  • Quotation
  • Sales Order
  • Requisition
  • Purchase Order
  • Goods Receipt
  • Goods Issue
  • Purchase Invoice
  • Maintenance Invoice
  • Payment
  • Receipt-Cum-Invoice
  • Other Charge

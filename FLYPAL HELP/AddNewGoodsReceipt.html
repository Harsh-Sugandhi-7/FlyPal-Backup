Add New Goods Receipt

You can add new Goods Receipt information on this screen. For adding new Goods Receipt information, go through following steps :-

How to Add New Goods Receipt ?

  1. Click Add New button on List of Receipts screen, You get Receipt Details screen.
  2. Enter all the information in Receipt Details screen & click Save button, The record is added successfully in the Receipt List.

Note:
The Receipt can not be save without Receipt Items.

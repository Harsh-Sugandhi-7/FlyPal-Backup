Edit Opening Balance

You can edit the Opening Balance information on this screen. For editing the Opening Balance, go through following steps :-

How To Edit The Opening Balance ?

  1. Click Edit link on the Part List screen, You get Part Information screen.
  2. Click Next button on Part Information screen, You get Applicable Model List For Part No. screen.
  3. Click Next button on Applicable Model List For Part No. screen, You get Opening Balance Information screen.
  4. Click Edit link on the selected record, You get Opening Stock Details screen.
  5. After editing click OK button, The updated record is added successfully in the Opening Balance List.
  6. Again click Close button & then click Save button to save the record.

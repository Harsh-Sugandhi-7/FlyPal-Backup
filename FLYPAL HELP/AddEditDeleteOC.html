Add/Edit/Delete Invoice Charge

To reach the Invoice Charge screen click Entries link, Select Other Charge link, You get List of Other Charge screen. Click Add New button on List of Other Charge screen, You get Other Charge Details screen. Again click Add button on Other Charge Details screen, You get Invoice Charge screen. 

How to Add New Charge ?

  1. Enter all the information & click Ok button, The record is added in the Charge List.
  2. After adding click Save button on Other Charge Details screen to save the record.

How to Edit The Charge ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record, You get Invoice Charge screen.
  2. After editing click Ok button, The updated record is added successfully in the Charge List.
  3. Again click Save button Other Charge Details screen to save the record.

How to Delete The Charge ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.
  3. After deleting click Save button to save the record.

Invoice Charge screen contains following fields :

Field Name Field Description
Vendor The name of the vendor. You can select it from drop down list.
Invoice No. It is the suppliers invoice number (e.g. ENP/DA/01).
Invoice Date Is the suppliers invoice date (e.g. 11-Aug-2004).
Charge Type It is the type of charge. You can select it from drop down list.
Currency It is the name of currency (e.g. Rupees).
Factor It is the factor of that selected currency (e.g. 1.00).
Charge Name It is the name of the charge (e.g. Service charge).
Charge Amount You can enter the charge amount of that invoice (e.g. 100).
Service Charge You can enter the service charge of that invoice (e.g. 200).
Total Amount Total Amount=Charge Amount + Service Charge (e.g. 100+200 = 300).

Invoice Charge screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Ok Ok button for adding the Charge in Charge List.
Back Back button to return back to the Other Charge Details screen.

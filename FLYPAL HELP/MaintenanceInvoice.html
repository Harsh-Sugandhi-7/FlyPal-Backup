Maintenance Invoice

To reach the List of Maintenance Invoice screen click Entries link, Select Maintenance Invoice link, You get List of Maintenance Invoice screen. You can Add/Edit/Delete/Search Maintenance Invoice information on this screen.

Maintenance Invoice Details screen contains following fields :

Field Name Field Description
Date The date on which the Maintenance Invoice is being made (e.g. 12-Mar-2006).
No. Maintenance Invoice Number is auto generated (e.g. EXP-2005-2006-1).
Name The name of vendor. You can select the it from drop down list (e.g. Indian Oil corporation Ltd).
Invoice No. It is the suppliers invoice number (e.g. VIN-153).
Invoice Date Invoice date is the suppliers invoice date (e.g. 11-Aug-2004).
Part Number The Part No. is an alphanumeric code to identify any part. It must be unique (e.g. 000-170000-23).
Serial No. When you select the serialized part at that time Serial No. is compulsory. It must be unique (e.g. SR-6267).
Charges For You can select the charges from drop down list. You can also add charges in it's alternate text box.
Quantity You can enter the quantity of that maintenance invoice. But when you select the serialized part at that time quantity is always taking 1.
Rate You can enter the rate of that maintenance invoice (e.g. 450.50).
Amount Amount=Quantity*Rate (e.g. 100.00*50.00=5000.00).
Other Charges You can enter the other charges of that maintenance invoice .
Grand Total Grand Total=Amount + Other Charges (e.g. 500.00+200.00=700.00).
Remark You can enter any remark for this entry in the maintenance invoice.

Maintenance Invoice Details screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Save Save button to save the Maintenance Invoice information.
Back Back button to return back to the List of Maintenance Invoice screen.

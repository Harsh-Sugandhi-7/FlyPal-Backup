Add New Other Charge

You can add new Other Charge information on this screen. For adding new Other Charge information, go through following steps :-

How to Add New Other Charge ?

  1. Click Add New button on List of Other Charge screen, You get Other Charge Details screen.
  2. Enter all the information in Other Charge Details screen & click Save button, The record is added successfully in the Other Charge List.

Note:
Other Charge can not be save without Invoice Items.

Delete Goods Receipt

You can delete the Goods Receipt information on this screen. For deleting the Goods Receipt information, go through following steps :-

How to Delete The Goods Receipt ?

  1. Search a particular record & click Find Now button. You can Search the Receipt from following way:
    • All : From this you search all the records of receipt.
    • Date : From this you can search the Receipt by Date. You can search the Receipt Date by All, Last 1 week, Last 1 Month, Last 1 Quarter, Last 1 year, Current Financial Year & Between Dates. For Between Dates criteria you have to enter the starting & ending dates of receipt.
    • Receipt : From this you search the Receipt by Receipt number. For searching this you have to select the Receipt Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search.
    • Internal Receipt No. : From this you search the Receipt by Internal Receipt No. For searching this you have to enter the Internal Receipt Number.
    • Part No. : From this you search the Receipt by Part Number. For searching this you have to enter the part number.
    • Vendor : From this you search the Receipt by Vendor Name. For searching this you have to enter the name of vendor. 
    • Order : From this you search the Receipt by Order No. For searching this you have to select the Order Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search.
    • DC No. : From this you search the Receipt by DC No. For searching this you have to enter the DC Number.
    • Release Note No.    : From this you search the Receipt by Release Note No. For searching this you have to enter the Release Note Number.
    • Status : From this you search the Receipt by Status. For searching this you have to select the status from drop down list. The status may be Opened, Authorized or Cancelled.
  2. Click Delete link which you want to delete the record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  3. Click Yes for deletion and No for cancel the action.

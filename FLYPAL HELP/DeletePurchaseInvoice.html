Delete Purchase Invoice

You can delete Purchase Invoice information on this screen. For deleting the Purchase Invoice information, go through following steps :-

How to Delete The Purchase Invoice ?

  1. Search a particular record & click Find Now button. You can Search the Invoice from following way :
    • All : From this you search all the records of Invoice.
    • Date : From this you can search the Invoice by Date. You can search the Invoice Date by All, Last 1 week, Last 1 Month, Last 1 Quarter, Last 1 year, Current Financial Year & Between Dates. For Between Dates criteria you have to enter the starting & ending dates of invoice.
    • Invoice : From this you search the Invoice by Invoice Number. For searching this you have to select the Invoice Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search. 
    • Part No. : From this you search the Invoice by Part Number. For searching this you have to enter the part number.
    • Vendor : From this you search the Invoice by Vendor Name. For searching this you have to enter the name of the Vendor.
    • Order : From this you search the Invoice by Order Number. For searching this you have to select the Order Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search. 
    • Receipt : From this you search the Invoice by Receipt number. For searching this you have to select the Receipt Text from drop down list & also enter the number which you want to search. 
    • Status : From this you search the Invoice by Status. For searching this you have to select the status from drop down list. The status may be Opened, Authorized or Cancelled.
  2. Click Delete link which you want to delete the record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  3. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

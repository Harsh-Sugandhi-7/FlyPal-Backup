Add/Delete Invoice Item

To reach the List of Invoices screen click Entries link, Select Other Charge link, You get List of Other Charge screen. Click Add New button on List of Other Charge screen, You get Other Charge Details screen. Again click Add button on Other Charge Details screen, You get List of Invoices screen. 

How to Add New Invoice ?

  1. For selecting the Invoice click the Checkbox on List of Invoices screen & click Ok button, The record is added successfully in the Invoice List.
  2. After adding click Save button on Other Charge Details screen to save the record.

How to Delete The Invoice ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected Invoice, The selected Invoice is getting deleted from the Invoice List.
  2. After deleting click Save button on Other Charge Details screen to save the record.

Other Charge Details screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Ok Ok button to add the Invoice in Invoice List.
Back Back button to return back to the Other Charge Details screen.

Note:

  • You can delete the Invoice only when the Other Charge is not saved.
  • You can also delete the Invoice from List of Invoices screen. For that Uncheck the selected checkbox.

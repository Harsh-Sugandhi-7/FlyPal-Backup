Delete Opening Balance

You can delete the Opening Balance information on this screen. For deleting the Opening Balance information, go through following steps :-

How To Delete The Opening Balance ?

  1. Click Edit link on the Part List screen, You get Part Information screen.
  2. Click Next button on Part Information screen, You get Applicable Model List For Part No. screen.
  3. Click Next button on Applicable Model List For Part No. screen, You get Opening Balance Information screen.
  4. Click Delete link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  5. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.
  6. After deleting click Close button & then Save button to save the record.

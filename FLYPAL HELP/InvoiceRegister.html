Invoice Register Report

To reach the Invoice Register Report click Registers link, Select Invoice Register link, You get Invoice Register Search Criteria screen. Select the criteria & click Display button, The searching report is display in the report screen. You can search the report from following ways :-

  • Date Range : From this you can search the Invoice by Date. You can search the Invoice Date by All, Last 1 week, Last 1 Month, Last 1 Quarter, Last 1 year, Current Financial Year & Between Dates. For Between Dates criteria you have to enter the starting & ending dates of Invoice.
  • Doc Type : From this you search the Receipt by Doc Type. The Doc Type may be Invoice, Receipt or Order. For searching this you have to enter Invoice/Receipt/Order No.
  • Vendor : From this you search the Invoice by Vendor name. For searching this you have to select the vendor from drop don list.
  • Status : From this you search the Invoice by Status. For searching this you have to select the status from drop down list. The status may be Opened, Authorized or Cancelled.
  • Part No./Description : From this you search the Invoice by Part Number or Description. For searching this you have to click Select link on Part List.

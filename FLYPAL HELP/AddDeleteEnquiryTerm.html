Add/Delete Enquiry Term

To reach the List of Enquiry Term screen click Entries link, Select Enquiry link, You get List of Enquiry screen. Click Add New button on List of Enquiry screen, You get Enquiry Details screen. Again click Add button on Enquiry Details screen, You get List of Enquiry Term screen. 

How to Add New Enquiry Term ?

  1. For selecting the Term click the Checkbox on List of Enquiry Term screen & click Ok button, The record is added successfully in the Enquiry Term List.
  2. After adding click Save button on Enquiry Details screen to save the record.

How to Delete The Enquiry Term ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected Term, The selected Term is getting deleted from the Enquiry Term List.
  2. After deleting click Save button on Enquiry Details screen to save the record.

List of Enquiry Term screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Ok Ok button to add the selected Terms in the Enquiry Term List.
Back Back button to return back to the Enquiry Details screen.

Note:
You can also delete the Terms from List of Enquiry Term screen. For that Uncheck the selected checkbox.

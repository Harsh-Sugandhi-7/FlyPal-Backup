Add/Edit/Delete Invoice Charge

To reach the Invoice Charge screen click Entries link, Select Purchase Invoice link, You get List of Purchase Invoice screen. Click Add New button on List of Purchase Invoice screen, You get Invoice Details screen. Again click Add button on Invoice Details screen, You get Invoice Charge screen. 

How to Add New Invoice Charge ?

  1. Enter all the information & click Ok button, The record is added in the Invoice Charge List.
  2. After adding click Save button on Invoice Details screen to save the record.

How to Edit The Invoice Charge ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record, You get Invoice Charge screen.
  2. After editing click Ok button, The updated record is added successfully in the Invoice Charge List.
  3. Again click Save button Invoice Details screen to save the record.

How to Delete The Invoice Charge ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.
  3. After deleting click Save button to save the record.

Invoice Charge screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Ok Ok button for adding the Invoice Charge in the List.
Back Back button to return back to the Invoice Details screen.

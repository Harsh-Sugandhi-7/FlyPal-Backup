Add/Edit/Delete Term

To reach the Term screen click Entries link, Select Enquiry link, You get List of Enquiry screen. Click Add New button on List of Enquiry screen, You get Enquiry Details screen. Click Add button on Enquiry Details screen, You get List of Enquiry Term screen. Again click on the Icon of Term field, You get Term screen.

How to Add New Term ?

  1. Enter the name of Term & click Add/Edit button to add the record in the list.

How to Edit The Term ?

  1. Click Edit link which you want to edit the record.
  2. After editing click Add/Edit button, The updated record is added successfully in the Term List.

How to Delete The Term ?

  1. Click Delete link on the selected Term, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.

Terms screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Add/Edit Add/Edit button to add/edit the term.
Back Back button to return back to the List of Enquiry Term screen.

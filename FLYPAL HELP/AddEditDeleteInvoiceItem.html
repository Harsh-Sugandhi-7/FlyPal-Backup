Add/Edit/Delete Invoice Item

To reach the Invoice Item screen click Entries link, Select Purchase Invoice link, You get List of Purchase Invoice screen. Click Add New button on List of Purchase Invoice screen, You get Invoice Details screen. Again click Add button on Invoice Details screen, You get Invoice Item screen.

How to Add New Invoice Item ?

  1. Enter all the information & click Ok button, The record is added successfully in the Invoice Item List.
  2. After adding click Save button on Invoice Details screen to save the current record.

How to Edit The Invoice Item ?

  1. Click Edit link on the selected record, You get Invoice Item screen.
  2. After editing click Ok button, The updated record is added in the Invoice Item List.
  3. Again click Save button on Invoice Details screen to save the current record.

How to Delete The Invoice Item ?

  1. Click Remove link on the selected record, You get a message box for confirmation.
  2. Click Yes for deletion & No for cancel the action.
  3. After deleting click Save button to save the current record.

Invoice Item Details screen contains following buttons :

Button Name Action
Ok Ok button to save the Invoice Items.
Back Back button to return back to the Invoice Details screen.

Invoice Item Details screen contains following fields :

Field Name Field Description
Quantity It is the balance quantity of that invoice . Quantity is editable but it never be greater than it's Invoice Balance quantity.
Rate You can enter the rate of that invoice item (e.g. 450.50).
Other Charges You can enter the other charges of that invoice item (e.g. 2000).
Amount Amount= Quantity*Rate + Other Charges (e.g. 10.00*10.00+100.00=200.00).
Effective Rate Effective Rate = Rate + ((Total Charges/Total Amount)*Rate).
Remark You can enter any remark for this entry in invoice item screen.
Note Any other information associated with that invoice item is entered in the note.

Note:
The fields of Invoice Item & Invoice Item Information are selected when you click on the Icon of Part No. field, You get List of Pending Invoice Items screen. Search a particular record & click Select link, You get Detail information of the Part. Again click Select link, The information of that part is added in the Invoice Item screen.

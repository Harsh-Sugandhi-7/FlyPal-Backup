Edit Maintenance Invoice

You can edit the Maintenance Invoice information on this screen. For editing the Maintenance Invoice information, go through following steps :-

How to Edit The Maintenance Invoice ?

  1. Search a particular record & click Find Now button. You can Search the Maintenance Invoice from following way :
    • All : From this you search all the records of Maintenance Invoice.
    • Entry Date : From this you can search the Maintenance Invoice by Date. For searching this you have to select the From Date & To Date from drop down list.
    • Charge For : From this you search the Maintenance Invoice by Charges. For searching this you have to select the charges from drop down list.
    • Vendor : From this you search the Maintenance Invoice by Vendor Name. For searching this you have to select the vendor name from drop down list.
    • Vendor Invoice Date : From this you can search the Maintenance Invoice by Vendor Invoice Date. For searching this you have to select the From Date & To Date from drop down list.
  2. Click Edit link which you want to edit the record, You get Maintenance Invoice Details screen.
  3. After editing click Save button, The updated record is added in the Maintenance Invoice List.
